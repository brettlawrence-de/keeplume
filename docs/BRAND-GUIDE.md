# Nelmavriq brand guide

Everything in its place. Pronunciation: NEL-ma-vrik.
Original geometric emblem: a stored cube within four location corners; copper point marks its remembered position. Created locally as SVG, independent of keeplume.com artwork.
Midnight navy #101E32; refined teal #087F83; restrained copper #B8754D; light ink #F2F7F8; dark teal #70CEC9. Copper is an accent, not body text. System Segoe UI/Arial/sans-serif keeps the app bundled and readable without external font requests.
Horizontal logo: wordmark.svg / wordmark-light.svg and wordmark-dark.svg. Emblem: emblem-light.svg and emblem-dark.svg. Navy app icon: icon.svg. Preserve aspect ratio, artwork and clear space of at least one cube-edge width; never stretch. Header width188px mobile/220px desktop; use emblem for very small contexts. App icon is separate from full wordmark.
Raster outputs: Apple180, PWA192/512, maskable512 (emblem inset inside central safe circle), favicon32/48. The public path, manifest identity and stored data remain unchanged.
Rename note is intentional historical disclosure; technical legacy identifiers remain for compatibility. No uniqueness/legal-clearance claim. See external REBRAND-PROGRESS research for sources and limitations.
